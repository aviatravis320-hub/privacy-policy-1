Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Vybez Recovery List ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application ("App").

By using our App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information You Provide:

  • Sobriety date and recovery milestones
  • Mood tracking data and journal entries
  • Prayer and meditation preferences
  • Emergency contact information you choose to save
  • Payment information for premium subscriptions (processed securely by Stripe)

Information Collected Automatically:

  • Device information (device type, operating system version)
  • App usage data and analytics
  • Crash reports and performance data
  • Approximate location (only when you use the Meeting Finder feature)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our services
  • Track and display your recovery progress
  • Personalize your app experience
  • Process subscription payments securely
  • Send important service notifications
  • Find nearby recovery meetings (when you grant location permission)
  • Respond to your support requests

4. Data Storage and Security

Your recovery data is primarily stored locally on your device for your privacy. For premium features, some data may be synced to our secure servers.

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Secure authentication protocols
  • Regular security assessments
  • Limited employee access to personal data

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Stripe: For secure payment processing. Stripe's privacy policy: stripe.com/privacy
  • Expo/React Native: For app functionality
  • Analytics: To improve app performance and user experience

These third-party services have their own privacy policies governing the use of your information.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Opt-out of analytics and non-essential data collection
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@vybezrecovery.com

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services. If you request account deletion, we will delete your data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

9. International Users

If you are accessing our App from outside the United States, please be aware that your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States where our servers are located.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the App and updating the "Last Updated" date above.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us:

Email: privacy@vybezrecovery.com

By using Vybez Recovery List, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.
